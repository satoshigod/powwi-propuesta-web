Two business lines, one entity

Electronic deposits and payments

As a SEDPE, Powwi takes funds from the public in electronic deposits and provides payments, transfers and remittances. It does not grant credit.

Foreign exchange

As a foreign exchange market intermediary, Powwi carries out exchange operations under Colombia's foreign exchange regime.
